Q: What is Euler's identity?
A: $$e^{i\pi} + 1 = 0$$
Extra: It links five fundamental constants: $e$, $i$, $\pi$, $1$ and $0$.

---

C: The sum $1 + 2 + \dots + n$ equals [$\frac{n(n+1)}{2}$].
Extra: Pair the first term with the last, the second with the second-to-last, and so on: each pair adds up to $n + 1$, and there are $n/2$ pairs.

Q: What are the solutions of $ax^2 + bx + c = 0$?
A: $$x = \frac{-b \pm \sqrt{b^2 - 4ac}}{2a}$$
Extra: The discriminant $b^2 - 4ac$ says how many real solutions there are: two if it is positive, one if it is zero, none if it is negative.

---

C: A prime number has exactly [two] distinct positive divisors.
Extra: That is why $1$ is not prime: it has only one.

Q: What is $\displaystyle\int_0^1 x^2\,dx$?
A: $\dfrac{1}{3}$
Extra: An antiderivative of $x^2$ is $\dfrac{x^3}{3}$.
